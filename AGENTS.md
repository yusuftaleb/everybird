# Everybird: agent instructions

Everybird turns any birder's life list into a sticker collage. Import an export (eBird, observation.org, iGoTerra, Scythebill, a spreadsheet) or an iNaturalist username, or tick birds by hand; filter, edit and share, as a collage, a list, a map or a tree. The list stays in the browser. It uses Yusuf's personal-site design system, and its collage page mirrors his /lifelist. Pages: My birds `/` (the Collage, List, Map and Tree views), Settings `/settings`, About `/about`, Compare `/compare`, shared view `/s/[token]`, shared comparison `/c/[token]`. Read these before any work:

- **Design system:** `DESIGN.md` (tokens, type, components, motion, voice, accessibility; sidecar `.impeccable/design.json`, product context in `PRODUCT.md`). Match it, and match `/lifelist`, for any UI work.
- **Spec (approved by Yusuf, 2026-11-05):** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-05-everybird-design.md`, with "Changes after approval" at its end.
- **Plan (25 tasks):** `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-05-everybird.md`
- **Validation:** `docs/validation.md` (launch checklist; aggregates only, no personal data).

The sppgroup endpoint has no version (it ignores `version=`), so family groups are the fetch day's; the manifest records a `sha256` per downloaded raw file. Each of the 26 `NAME_LOCALES` sets must have exactly the English taxonomy's species codes; Dutch `nl` is fetched as `nl_NL`, Polish `pl` as `pl` (eBird's near-complete global set, maintained by Sławomir Karpicki-Ignatowski), French `fr_FR` (France's names, the French base) and `fr` (eBird's plain French, North American usage, for Canada; eBird has no `fr_BE` or `fr_CH`, both return `fr`) German `de` (one set: `de_AT` and `de_CH` return it), Italian `it`, Swedish `sv` and Danish `da`. Raw taxonomy and groups, including English groups for exact family matching, are git-ignored and recorded with fetch dates in `data/raw/manifest.json`. `npx tsx scripts/build-data/build-names.ts` writes `public/data/names.<locale>.v1.json` (`{ v: 1, locale, taxonomy, names, fam }`), regional → base fallbacks baked in, names normalised (NFC, NBSP and runs of whitespace to one space, trimmed), names equal to English in any case omitted (the English reference is the raw `ebird-taxonomy.json`'s `comName` for the published codes; eBird fills a missing pt_BR/pt_PT name with the English one in lower case: about 8,000 of them, so pt covers 3,065 of 11,201 species, es and nl about 11,180, pl 11,197), every locale's names with their first letter capitalised (`capFirst`; the rest, inner Title Case included, as eBird wrote it). Each name is eBird's first; for the sets in `IOC_COLUMNS` (`it`, `sv`, `da`), where eBird only repeats the English name, the IOC World Bird List v15.2 name (Gill, Donsker & Rasmussen, CC BY 3.0) from huashu's `data/names.csv` at the pinned commit, through build-species' crosswalk (`iocNames`: the species' own huashu record, never a form); English last (left out of the file). Italian gets most of its names this way (eBird's `it` has few of its own); About's taxonomy credit names IOC in those three languages. A missing raw file stops the build naming `fetch-sources.ts --names-only`. Family labels are sentence case in every language (proper nouns and, in Dutch, proper adjectives keep their capital; German keeps every noun's). The hand-kept family labels in `data/i18n/families.{es,pt,nl,pl,fr,de,it,sv,da}.json` are **LLM translations awaiting fluent-speaker review**, including explicit cognates identical to English; hand labels win, then exact English group matches, except a group equal to the family's scientific name, containing any …idae name or a "(" (`usableGroupName`), else English is omitted and the build warns, listing the families. Hand labels also replace eBird's Title Case groups. There are 146/120/161/91/105/95/250/89/120 hand labels respectively (es/pt/nl/pl/fr/de/it/sv/da) for the 250 families in taxonomy 2026; eBird's Polish groups label the other 159 Polish families, and eBird has no Italian groups. `tests/build-names.test.ts` requires every family labelled, labels unique per language, no brackets or scientific names, no capital inside a label outside its `PROPER` allow-list, and at least 200 names of its own in each regional es_XX file. ## Smart search groundwork (item 9, A and C)

- `npx tsx scripts/build-data/build-groups.ts` GETs eBird species groups for every language eBird has them in (`GROUP_LOCALES`: en, es, pt_BR, nl, pl, fr, de, sv, da, ru, zh_SIM, zh; none for Italian or Japanese) (public endpoint; optional `EBIRD_API_KEY` stays in the header), validates localized bounds against the committed species taxonomy, and writes `public/data/groups.v1.json` (`{v:1,taxonomy,groups:[{id,name:{en,es,pt,nl,pl,fr,de,sv,da},lo,hi}]}`; inclusive bounds). Included in `npm run data`. Rerun `data-files.ts` after rebuilding. Never upload until review and Yusuf's approval. Group IDs are English-name slugs; `data/i18n/group-synonyms.json` maps everyday words to these groups (broad aliases such as raptors/warblers/sparrows select the named eBird group, not every family that might be called that).
- Saved v5 adds optional `SourceRecord.seenMonths` and `Unmatched.seenMonths`: one string per place, `<months>|region[|county]`, where `<months>` packs one five-character chunk per year (year as three base-36 digits, the 12-bit month mask as two base-64 digits, bit 0 = January; years ascending), written by `SeenAgg.getMonths`. The earlier `YYYY-MM,YYYY-MM|region` shape still decodes. MyEBirdData and iNaturalist fill it; fixed unmatched rows retain it. `decodeSeenMonths` decodes it. Migration does not synthesize month history for v1–v4; absent is legacy/life-list-only, `[]` means a full source had no dated, located sighting. Existing `buildSeen` and filtering still use earliest dates; part B must consume the month sets and implement the fallback.
- `src/lib/search/smart.ts`: pure `parseSmart(q, ctx)` and `withoutChip(q, chip)`. `SmartFilter = Partial<Filter & SmartFilterFields>` adds only a type contract for group/basis/statuses, no filter behavior. Context takes the clock, the UI language (`SearchLang` = `Lang`), group rows, regions, list place keys, optional localized status labels, and optional on-list species/order/family labels. Dates emit inclusive YYYY-MM; last N months means N calendar months including this month. Quotes force name text. Scalar dates/group/taxonomy take the first recognized match; status/place sets union. Chips carry exact source offsets and tokens; reparse after editing or removing one. Remaining words retain their spelling and punctuation.
- `src/lib/places/names.ts` is the single home for the shared collision-safe country map, sub1 index, and observation.org alias tables (re-exported by `src/lib/import/sub1-index.ts` and `src/lib/import/obsorg-places.ts`), plus country abbreviations and Portuguese Intl names.
- Part D (DockSearch, done): the query's group, place, period, Red List status and "lifers" show as "Understood as" chips (`ChipRow`, the filter's chip; order and family words stay bird-name hits, as before). Enter, or "Show these birds" (both only when chips show; a highlighted hit wins over Enter), applies them in one `{type:'filter'}` through `applySmart` (`src/lib/search/apply.ts`): a search replaces every filter: `applySmart` starts from nothing and sets the kinds the shown chips hold (places as one dimension, through `withPlaces`), so no earlier filter, order or family included, stacks on it. Typing applies nothing. Name hits search the query as typed, or, with chips and no hit, what the chips leave over (`nameQuery`). `groups.v1.json` loads once when the search opens (`fetchData`; a failure is not cached). With no per-place months on the user's own list (`hasMonths`), a dates chip shows `ui.search.note`. Strings: `ui.search` in all four languages. Part B (`group` and `basis` matching, the Time switch, the card's first sighting inside a filter) is in too (see the filter model above).
